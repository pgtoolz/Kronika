Hour-picker focus probe

Standalone reproduction of the three relevant fragments of src/hour-picker.tsx: the onBlur guard (lines 101-104), the pointerdown capture dismissal (lines 51-54, 61) and the requestAnimationFrame(...focus()) call (line 50). React 19 attaches event listeners to the portal container, so the popover below gets its own focusout listener to emulate propagation through the React tree.

Open this file in Chrome/Firefox and in Safari, click open, then click day. In Blink/Gecko the day click fires; in WebKit the popover closes before the click arrives. The page also exposes window.probeState() for automated runs (Playwright page.click("#trigger") then page.click("#day")).
